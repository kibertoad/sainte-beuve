Each finding of a parked AI review is picked with a checkbox that says what checking it does:
**Post** when the review settles with comments on the pull request, **Fix** when cat-factory
fixes it. An unchecked finding reads **Skipped** or **Ignored**, and the footer's tally uses the
same words, instead of a toggle button reading **Left out**.
