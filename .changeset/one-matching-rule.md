One matching rule. An attention request nobody was named on reaches whoever holds at least half of what it wants (`ATTENTION_FIT_FLOOR`), measured as the share of wanted skills and domains that also ranks the recommendations and weights the board's draw. An ask now captures what it wants when it is raised: the picked skills, else the pull request's board row's, else the repository's, and the repository's domains. `AttentionRequest` gains `domains`, defaulting to none for requests stored before.

`@sainte-beuve/reviewers` replaces `hasAllSkills` with `affinityOf` and `fitsWanted`, and `AttentionAudienceRule.requiredSkills` with `wanted: MatchTarget`.
